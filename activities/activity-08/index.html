Sunset Beach

Sunset Beach has a long shoreline and great views at the end of the day. Winter swells can bring big surf, and calmer seasons are perfect for walks and relaxing by the water.

Banzai Pipeline (Ehukai)

Pipeline is one of the most famous surf breaks in the world. Waves break over a shallow reef and form fast barrels. It's a popular place to watch experienced surfers from the sand.

Waimea Bay

Waimea Bay is a wide, beautiful beach with a deep blue bay. In summer it can be calm for swimming and snorkeling. In winter the waves can get powerful and the surf is world-famous.

What kind of photos are good to encode using JPEG?

JPEG is best for photographs and images with many colors and gradients. It works well for realistic photos like landscapes, portraits, and nature scenes.

What kind of photos are good to encode using GIF?

GIF is best for simple graphics with limited colors, such as logos, icons, and simple animations.

What is unique about the PNG format?

PNG uses lossless compression, meaning no image quality is lost when saving. It supports millions of colors and full alpha transparency, allowing for smooth transparent backgrounds.

How do you resize your photo to the size it should be displayed?

Open the image, select the resize or image size option, enter the desired dimensions in pixels, and save the file.

Why should you resize photos using photo editing software instead of resizing it using CSS?

Resizing with photo editing software reduces the file size, which makes pages load faster. When you resize with CSS, the browser still downloads the full-size image and then scales it down.
