HUKI SHOW

What makes Huki truly unique is its presentation.
Live musicians set the enchanting tone as native performers tell our epic tale, all while floating upon the serene waters of the lagoon on large, double-hulled canoes.
It’s a visual and auditory feast that you won’t want to miss.

In Hawaiian, Huki means to pull. This powerful theme runs through the show, from the legendary story of Maui pulling up the islands, to the cherished history of hukilau in Lāʻie, where fishing nets brought the community together.
Huki beautifully illustrates that the ocean, far from dividing us, unites us as one ʻohana (family), sharing the spirit of aloha.
