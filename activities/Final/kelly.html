Kelly Slater

11× WSL World Champion — The Greatest of All Time

Biography

Photo: Courtesy of Todd Glasser [Source]

Robert Kelly Slater, born on February 11, 1972, in Cocoa Beach, Florida, is widely regarded as the greatest professional surfer who has ever lived. With an unprecedented 11 World Surf League Championship titles, Slater has dominated his sport in a way that few athletes in any discipline have ever matched. [Impact our Future — Kelly Slater]

Slater grew up surfing the waves of Cocoa Beach and turned professional at just 18 years old. He won his first World Title in 1992 at the age of 20, becoming the youngest world champion in the history of professional surfing.

What makes Slater's story even more remarkable is his longevity. After a brief retirement, he returned to the tour and won his 11th and final (to date) World Title in 2011 at the age of 39, making him also the oldest world champion in WSL history. No other athlete holds both the youngest and oldest champion records in the same sport.

Career Highlights

Early Career and First Titles (1990–1998)

Slater joined the World Surf League Championship Tour in 1990. By 1992, he had accumulated enough points to claim his first World Title, defeating veteran surfers who had been on tour for years. He then had five consecutive world titles from 1994 to 1998. That streak of dominance seems impossible to beat.

The Comeback (2005–2011)

After stepping back from full-time competition, Slater returned in the tour in 2003. Between 2005 and 2008, he claimed four more back-to-back world championships, extending his record to ten titles. Then, after a two-year gap, he won his 11th title in 2011.

Pipeline Mastery

The Billabong Pipe Masters at Pipeline, Hawaii, is considered the most prestigious event on the WSL tour. Slater has won this event eight times. Pipeline is a dangerous and technically wave that breaks over a shallow reef, and Slater's ability to tube-ride it with flawless precision set him apart from all other competitors.

Perfect Scores

In 2005, at the Rip Curl Pro Search in Mundaka, Spain, Slater became the first surfer in WSL history to score a perfect 20 out of 20 in a heat (two perfect 10-point rides). Then he achieved multiple perfect 10-point single-wave scores throughout his career.

Legacy and Influence

Beyond the numbers, Kelly Slater's impact on surfing extends far beyond the competition arena. He helped bring professional surfing into the mainstream, appearing on the covers of global magazines, collaborating with major brands, and even making cameos in popular TV shows. He co-founded the sustainable surfwear brand Outerknown and has been a vocal advocate for ocean conservation and environmental issues.

His surfing style is a mix of flowing carves, powerful turns, and effortless tube riding. He influenced an entire generation of professional surfers including me. Many of today's top competitors, including Gabriel Medina, have cited Slater as their primary inspiration.
