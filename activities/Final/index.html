About the WSL

The World Surf League (WSL), is the main association for professional surfing worldwide. It was founded in 1976. The WSL runs the elite Championship Tour (CT), where the world's best surfers compete all around the globe. From Pipeline in Hawaii to Bells Beach in Australia to Teahupo'o in Tahiti. [WSL — About]

Each year, surfers accumulate points across a series of events to crown a World Champion. A surfer's ranking is determined by their best results throughout the season. There has been extraordinary rivalries, record-breaking performances, and careers that have left a permanent mark on the sport. [Wikipedia — World Surf League]

The five surfers featured on this site stand above all others in terms of titles won, consistency, influence, and raw talent. Together, they hold a combined 20 WSL World Titles ! This shows their dominance on professional surfing.

A Brief History of Professional Surfing

Professional surfing started in the 1960s with contest in Hawaii and California.

In 1983, the ASP (Association of Surfing Professionals) took over and expanded the tour globally. The circuit grew to include events at legendary surf spots around the world: the Banzai Pipeline in Hawaii, Bells Beach in Australia, Teahupo'o in Tahiti, and Jeffreys Bay in South Africa, among many others.

In 2015, the ASP was rebranded as the World Surf League (WSL), with a new era of media coverage, athlete pay, and global visibility for the sport. Today, the WSL Championship Tour consists of roughly 10 events per year for both men and women, culminating in a season-ending Title Race that determines the year's World Champion.
