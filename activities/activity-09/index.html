Welcome to the Tahiti Surf Tour

Discover my favorites surfing spots in Tahiti and French Polynesia! From beginner-friendly waves to the world-famous barrels of Teahupoo. Whether you're just starting out or you're a professional, Tahiti has the perfect wave waiting for you. Take a look at what I consider the best spot for you!

Tour Stops

Explore our surf spots organized by skill level:

  • Beginner - Orofara & Papenoo Bay Perfect for learning with gentle, forgiving waves
  • Intermediate - Embouchure Papara Build your skills on consistent, medium-sized waves
  • Advanced - Sapinus & Haapiti Challenge yourself with powerful reef breaks
  • Pro - Temae Test your limits on world-class waves
  • Legend - Teahupoo The heaviest wave on Earth - for experts only
