Welcome to the Tahiti Surf Tour

Discover the legendary surf spots of Tahiti and French Polynesia! From gentle beginner-friendly waves to the world-famous barrels of Teahupoo, this tour takes you through the most iconic surfing destinations in the South Pacific. Whether you're just starting out or you're a seasoned professional, Tahiti has the perfect wave waiting for you. Join us on this journey through crystal-clear waters and unforgettable surf experiences.

Tour Stops

Explore our surf spots organized by skill level:

  • Beginner - Orofara & Papenoo Bay Perfect for learning with gentle, forgiving waves
  • Intermediate - Embouchure Papara Build your skills on consistent, medium-sized waves
  • Advanced - Sapinus & Haapiti Challenge yourself with powerful reef breaks
  • Pro - Temae Test your limits on world-class waves
  • Legend - Teahupoo The heaviest wave on Earth - for experts only
