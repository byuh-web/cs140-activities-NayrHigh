Intermediate Surf Spot

Intermediate Level

Ready to take your surfing to the next level? Embouchure Papara is a shore break that offers small to medium waves (2-8 feet). Perfect to try bigger waves and stay out of the reef.

Embouchure Papara

Located at the river mouth in Papara, this spot offers consistent waves. The waves here are more powerful but still forgiving to practice new maneuvers. Excellent place to work on your bottom turns and learn to read more complex wave patterns.

The best time to go surf is in the morning when winds are calm. The wave offers both left and right options, allowing you to practice on your preferred side. On good days watch out for localism! Locals will fight you if you don't respect them and the spot.
