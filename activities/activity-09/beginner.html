Beginner Surf Spots

Beginner Level

Starting your surfing journey in Tahiti? These two spots offer the perfect conditions for beginners. This is where I learned how to surf growing up.

Orofara

20 minutes away from where I live is this beautiful black sand beach. Familly gather here with their kids to teach them how to swim and surf. Waves are usually 2 to 4 feet tall, perfect for the foamy longboard.

Papenoo Bay

This is my home spot. The bay is huge and gives a lot of space for beginner to come. Wave can be massive but they are usually super small, around 2 to 5 feet, because it's a little deep. No risk for you to get pounded on the ground.
