Pro Surf Spot

Pro Level

Temae is where serious surfers come to test their skills. Only surfers with years of experience should attempt this break.

Temae

Temae is a popular reef break located on the northeast coast of Moorea. The wave offers long, hollow barrels. On big days, Temae is one of the most exciting waves in French Polynesia. Look at that!

Local tip: Temae works best on a south to southwest swell with light offshore winds. The paddle out can be challenging, so make sure you have the fitness and experience to handle strong currents.
