Pro Surf Spot

Pro Level

Temae is where serious surfers come to test their skills against world-class waves. This spot on Moorea offers powerful, challenging conditions that separate the experienced from the truly skilled. Only surfers with years of experience should attempt this break.

Temae

Temae is a renowned right-hand reef break located on the northeast coast of Moorea. The wave offers long, hollow barrels that peel along the reef with incredible speed and power. When the swell is up, Temae transforms into one of the most exciting waves in French Polynesia.

The break is located near the old airport and is accessible by boat or a long paddle. The takeoff zone is critical - being in the right position when the set arrives makes all the difference. The wave can hold double overhead swells and produces some of the best barrels outside of Teahupoo.

Local tip: Temae works best on a south to southwest swell with light offshore winds. The paddle out can be challenging, so make sure you have the fitness and experience to handle strong currents.
