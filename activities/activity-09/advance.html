Advanced Surf Spots

Advanced Level

For experienced surfers ready to tackle more challenging waves, Sapinus and Haapiti deliver powerful reef breaks that demand respect and skill. These spots offer hollow waves, faster sections, and the opportunity to push your surfing to new heights.

Sapinus

A fast, hollow reef break that rewards confident surfers with excellent barrel opportunities. The wave breaks over shallow reef, so proper positioning and experience are essential. Best surfed at mid to high tide.

Haapiti

Located on the island of Moorea, Haapiti offers a powerful left-hand wave that peels along the reef. This spot is known for its consistent swell and challenging conditions. The stunning backdrop of Moorea's mountains makes every session unforgettable.

Both spots require solid ocean awareness and the ability to handle powerful waves. Make sure you're comfortable with reef breaks and have experience reading wave sets before attempting these locations.
