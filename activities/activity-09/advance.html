Advanced Surf Spots

Advanced Level

For experienced surfers ready to challenge the reef. Sapinus and Haapiti are powerful reef breaks that demand respect and skill. These spots offer hollow waves and have faster sections.

Sapinus

A fast, hollow reef where you can get crazy barrel. The wave breaks over a shallow reef, so proper positioning and experience are essential.

Haapiti

Located on the island of Moorea, Haapiti offers a powerful left. This spot is known for its consistent swell and challenging conditions.

Both spots require solid ocean awareness and the ability to handle powerful waves. Make sure you're comfortable with reef breaks and have experience reading wave sets before attempting those spots.
